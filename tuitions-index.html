Our Story

Built on eight years
of results

Nexa Tuitions started in 2016 with a single batch of 12 students and one core belief — that quality teaching, delivered with patience and rigour, changes outcomes.

Today, we serve over 1,200 students across Hyderabad, with faculties spanning mathematics, sciences, and humanities. Our alumni have gone on to the IITs, NITs, AIIMS, and top universities globally.

We keep our batches small intentionally. We track progress obsessively. We take our students' futures personally.
